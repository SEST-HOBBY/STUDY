## Descriptive claims tied to R1 to R10

1. C1300-12XS "twelve 10 Gbps-capable ports, including shared combo-port options" (6.1). Supported. The data sheet lists the C1300-12XS as "10 x 10G SFP+ + 2 x 10G copper/SFP+ combo + 1 x GE OOB management", summarised as "10 x 10 Gigabit SFP+; 2 x 10 Gigabit copper ports (combo with 2 x SFP+); 1 x GE management port". That is 12 usable data ports (the two combo ports each offer an RJ-45 or an SFP+ cage, not both), plus a separate management port. The draft's warning not to count 14 ports is correct. Switching capacity 240 Gbps. The Telecom Shop listing describes the same unit as "12 SFP+ ports, 2 shared 10GE uplinks".
2. C1300-12XS "the pair supports front-panel stacking" (6.1). Supported. The data sheet lists C1300-12XS in "C1300 Family 2" for hardware stacking, "Front Panel Stacking for up to eight switches", and "PIDs from the same Family can be stacked together. Cross-stacking between Families is not supported." Two 12XS units are the same PID, so they stack.
3. C1300-48P-4X and C1300-24P-4X PoE for phones and APs (6.2). Supported. Data sheet: 48P-4X has 48 x 30 W PoE+ ports with a 375 W budget and 4 x 10G SFP+; 24P-4X has 24 PoE+ ports with a 195 W budget and 4 x 10G SFP+. PB Tech's page also prints "PoE Budget 375 Watts" for the 48P-4X. Both are in stacking Family 1 (relevant only if they are ever stacked; the draft does not stack them).
4. C1300-24T-4X as DMZ switch (6.2, cited to R30, outside this batch). The data sheet confirms the model exists: 24 x 10/100/1000 ports, 4 x 10G SFP+, rack-mountable. Price not checked here.
5. FG-70G-BDL-950-12 "listing shows $2,038 and is out of stock" (6.3). Half supported. "Out of Stock" is exactly what the page says today. The price printed is $3,028.00, not $2,038; the draft figure looks like a digit transposition. The page does not say whether $3,028 includes GST. The $2,250 GST-inclusive allowance per site no longer covers the listed bundle.
6. FortiGate 70G provides "internet security, IPS, DMZ separation and VPN termination" with "UTP subscription and support" (6.3). Supported at the level the Fortinet page gives: it names the FortiGate 70G, links its data sheet, and describes the UTP bundle, the Intrusion Prevention Service and IPsec VPN. The Technology Centre listing also lists Intrusion Prevention, SSL inspection, antivirus, URL filtering and 10 x RJ-45 ports for the 70G. The draft's advice to check throughput with inspection enabled is consistent with both pages.
7. Dell R360 "starting price of $6,047.97 including GST" (6.5). Not verifiable from here. Dell blocks automated reads. Search-index snippets of Dell AU pages show lower starting figures ($3,179.66 inc GST and delivery for order code per36010a). The sentence should be re-checked by the student or softened; the $18,000 configured allowance does not depend on it.
8. Windows Server 2025 Standard 16-core at $1,744.45 (6.5 licensing, 6.9). Supported exactly, in stock, AUD inc GST.
9. User CAL at $109.45 (6.5 licensing, 6.9). Not verifiable (Dell blocked). The nearest Australian retail listing found is Techbuy EP2-25261 at $98.65 inc GST (backorder) or the EP2-25279 five-pack at $470.55 inc GST ($94.11 each, in stock). Techbuy's one-licence page does not print the word "User"; the identification rests on the Microsoft part number (EP2-25261 is the Windows Server 2025 1 User CAL; PB Tech lists the same part as "Windows Server 2025 User CAL") and on the five-pack's stock code "SMS-WINSVR25-5CU-CAL". Do not confuse with the Device CAL EP2-25225 at $69.55.
10. R9 Microsoft pricing page. The draft does not attach a figure to it; the page shows none ("VARIABLE"). It does support the two licensing statements the draft makes: Standard is sold as 16-core packs, and CALs are required for every user or device.
